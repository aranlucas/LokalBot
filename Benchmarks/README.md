# Benchmarks

These are the measurements behind LokalBot's default models. Each report records the exact model files and revisions, runtime versions, test set, and known limits; the harness that produced it sits in the same folder so the run can be repeated. Unless a report says otherwise, every run used one Apple M4 Max with 48 GB of unified memory. No 16 GB, M1, M2, or M3 results have been measured yet.

## Not adopted

These alternatives were measured against what the app uses and did not replace it. Rejections already noted in the table above, such as Whisper for Serbian, are not repeated here.

| Role | Tried | Compared with | Result | Report |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reading text aloud | Breeze TTS 2 `Q8_0` on audio.cpp | Kokoro 82M, the app's current voice | One listener, not blinded, found Breeze more natural. It took 63.1 vs 27.0 s for a 255-word passage and peaked at 6.4 vs 0.8 GiB. Once loaded, its first audio arrived in 0.84–1.12 s; Kokoro returns only the finished file. Product use needs a separate commercial license. | [2026-09-09](Breeze/2026-09-09/REPORT.md) |
| Transcription and speakers | FunASR: Qwen3-ASR 1.7B on PyTorch with FSMN VAD and CAM++ speakers | The app's Qwen3-ASR with Silero VAD and Pyannote, before Nemotron | On 270 s of AMI meeting audio: 3.2× slower, 31.7% vs 28.3% WER, 43.6% vs 36.6% DER, and a 10.1 vs about 7.0 GiB peak. Its speaker-attributed WER was lower (63.7% vs 66.3%). Word alignment helped both: 53.1% on the app's transcript, 49.8% on FunASR's. | [2026-09-08](FunASR/2026-09-08/REPORT.md) |
| Ask or Search for a typed query | Three Laya 0.3.20 classifier checkpoints and their language router | The Swift `AskIntent` rules | On 240 English, Serbian Latin and Serbian Cyrillic queries, the best Laya setup got 71.7% right vs 60.0%, but sent 18.3% of searches to Ask vs 9.2% and failed 4 of 12 existing test cases. Swapping the order of the two options changed 14–38 of 240 answers. The bar was 95% accuracy and at most 2% of searches sent to Ask. | [2026-09-24](Laya/results/2026-09-24/REPORT.md) |
| Meeting actions and tool calls | MiniCPM5 2B `Q4_K_M`, with `Q8_0` as a check | Qwen3.5 4B `Q4_K_M` | 7/12 short extraction cases right vs 9/12, at 0.28 vs 0.55 s median; `Q8_0` reached 8/12. It made owner, timestamp and Serbian Cyrillic errors. Tool calls worked (5/6 vs 6/6) with a 42% smaller server footprint. This follows the 2026-09-07 pilot cited above. | [2026-09-10](MiniCPM5/2026-09-10/REPORT.md) |
